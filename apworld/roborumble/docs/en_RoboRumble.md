# RoboRumble

## What does randomization do to this game?

The weapons (robot heads), legs and alien boosters you can build robots from are no longer handed out per mission;
they are items. Missions are unlocked by Mission Access items and can be played in any order, like a mission map.

## What is the goal?

Win the final mission against the Aliens (mission 16).

## Which locations are checks?

- winning each of the 16 missions of your side
- building a robot with each of your side's weapons and legs for the first time
- building a robot with each booster for the first time (option `booster_builds`)

## What does another world's item look like in RoboRumble?

A message in the game; the parts appear in the robot builder as soon as they arrive.

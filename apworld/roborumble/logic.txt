# RoboRumble mission logic: what you need before logic sends you into a mission.
#
# First draft from the Steam walkthrough by m00f (steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=779093375).
# Edit freely; the apworld reads this file when a seed is generated and stops with the line number if
# something is written wrong.
#
# How to write it:
#   == Red Star Mission 1 ==     starts a mission (Red Star or Terraflux, 1-16)
#   base: no                     the mission has no factory (nothing can be built there, so it gives no
#                                build checks). Leave the line out when there is one.
#   need: A | B                  one requirement: A or B. Every "need" line of a mission must be met.
#   need: A & B | C              "&" ties things together: (A and B) or C
#   recommended: ...             like "need", but only with logic_strictness: normal (left out with relaxed)
#   # ...                        a comment
#
# Names: any weapon, legs, booster, base upgrade or invention by its item name (upper/lower case doesn't
# matter). "2-Legs" means the side's own 2-Legs. Some shortcuts:
#   damage weapon      any weapon of the side that does damage (not Oiler, Nitrogrenade or Disrupter)
#   any weapon / any legs
#   2 damage weapons   a number in front asks for that many different ones (works for all three shortcuts)
#   2x Progressive Starting Robo Funds   "Nx" in front of an item asks for that many copies of it (each Robo
#                      Funds item: +400 Robo Funds, the money for building robots, at the start of a mission)
#
# Unholy Alliance (option) can give a side the other side's parts: then a part named here that the side doesn't
# get counts as "damage weapon", "any weapon" (Oiler, Nitrogrenade, Disrupter) or "any legs" instead.
#
# Red Star weapons: Chain Gun, Rocket Launcher, Homing Missile, Mine Spreader, Oiler, Magneton, Force Field, Nuke
# Red Star legs:    2-Legs A, Heavy 4-Legs, Kangaroo, Caterpillar, 4-Tires, Amphibian, Propeller, Snail
# Terraflux weapons: Laser, Pulse Gun, Eraser, Buzzer, Nitrogrenade, Disrupter, Whirlwind, Infector
# Terraflux legs:    2-Legs B, Teleporter, Jet-Pack, Spider Legs, Jet-Car, Hovercraft, Antigraviton, Cube
# Boosters: Speed Booster, Turn Speed Booster, Fire Power Booster, Fire Range Booster, Fire Frequency Booster,
#           Shield, Camouflage, Maximum Weapon Booster, Maximum Defense Booster
# Base upgrades: Faster Production, Cheaper Production, Base Energy +10%, Base Regeneration,
#                Stronger Energy Explosions, Wider Energy Explosions, Explosions Recharge Robos,
#                Enemy Explosions Spare 1%
# Inventions: Robo Intelligence Enhancer, Robo Spawn Protection, Robo Auto-Repair
# Other: Progressive Starting Robo Funds


# ======================================================================== RED STAR

== Red Star Mission 1 ==
# The enemy waits until you come close. Guide: 5x Rocket Launcher with Shield or Fire Frequency.
# Hidden rule (game code): the mission is lost when 3 of your robos are destroyed.
need: damage weapon
recommended: Rocket Launcher | Chain Gun

== Red Star Mission 2 ==
# Destroy the base. Guide: 1x Rocket Launcher/Speed sneaks to the base, 3x Homing Missile/Shield defend.
need: Rocket Launcher | Homing Missile
recommended: Speed Booster | Shield

== Red Star Mission 3 ==
# Only one robo type allowed; steal the alien boosters. Guide: 5x Rocket Launcher/2-Legs/Speed.
need: Rocket Launcher | Homing Missile
recommended: Speed Booster
recommended: Faster Production

== Red Star Mission 4 ==
# Destroy the whole convoy, spare the prototype. Guide: enough Mine Spreaders; something cheap to set mines off.
need: Mine Spreader
recommended: Chain Gun | Homing Missile

== Red Star Mission 5 ==
# Break a strong coastal line; the enemy never attacks. Guide: Chain Guns, Mine Spreaders/4-Legs/Shield,
# Mine Spreaders or Magnetons across the water, Homing Missiles or Rocket Launchers for the base.
need: Mine Spreader | Magneton
need: Rocket Launcher | Homing Missile
recommended: Chain Gun
recommended: Heavy 4-Legs

== Red Star Mission 6 ==
# $550: only time for one robo. Guide: Homing Missile or Rocket Launcher/4-Legs/Maximum Weapon Booster,
# collect the two alien boosters.
need: Rocket Launcher | Homing Missile
recommended: Maximum Weapon Booster | Fire Power Booster
recommended: Heavy 4-Legs

== Red Star Mission 7 ==
# Two enemy bases, the second behind a locked door. Guide: 4x Rocket Launcher/4-Tires/Speed, then Mine
# Spreaders/Magnetons/Chain Guns/Rocket Launchers against the onslaught.
need: Rocket Launcher | Homing Missile
need: Mine Spreader | Magneton | Chain Gun
recommended: 4-Tires
recommended: Speed Booster

== Red Star Mission 8 ==
# Guide: a blocker (Oiler/Caterpillar/Maximum Defense) and 2x Homing Missile/4-Tires/Maximum Weapon Booster.
need: Homing Missile | Rocket Launcher
recommended: Oiler & Caterpillar
recommended: Maximum Weapon Booster | Fire Power Booster

== Red Star Mission 9 ==
# The enemy builds robos with alien boosters. Guide: 1x Homing Missile/4-Legs/Maximum Weapon collects the
# alien boosters; Oilers, Mine Spreaders, Rocket Launchers and Homing Missiles defend; buy base regeneration.
need: Homing Missile
need: Mine Spreader | Rocket Launcher | Oiler
recommended: Maximum Weapon Booster
recommended: Base Regeneration | Base Energy +10%

== Red Star Mission 10 ==
# Floating fortress, endless hovercraft attacks. Guide: Force Fields through the fortress' openings
# (Force Field/Caterpillar/Maximum Defense + Force Field/Amphibian/Speed), Homing Missile/Amphibian/Maximum Weapon.
need: Force Field | Homing Missile
recommended: Force Field & Amphibian
recommended: Caterpillar
recommended: Maximum Defense Booster | Maximum Weapon Booster

== Red Star Mission 11 ==
# Three bases sending "indestructible" cubes. Guide: 3x Rocket Launcher/4-Tires/Speed on the switches,
# Mine Spreader/4-Tires/Speed against the cubes, a cheap Chain Gun to set the mines off.
need: Rocket Launcher | Homing Missile
need: Mine Spreader | Chain Gun
recommended: 4-Tires
recommended: Speed Booster

== Red Star Mission 12 ==
# No money: herd the fleeing enemies into the Force Field you are given.
base: no

== Red Star Mission 13 ==
# Guide: 3x Nuke/4-Tires/Speed blow up the Erasers, 4x Force Field/4-Tires/Speed cut through the base.
need: Force Field
need: Nuke | Homing Missile
recommended: 4-Tires
recommended: Speed Booster

== Red Star Mission 14 ==
# Guide: 4x Force Field/4-Tires/Speed, lay a Force Field through the base.
need: Force Field
recommended: 4-Tires
recommended: Speed Booster

== Red Star Mission 15 ==
# Guide: 6x Force Field/4-Tires/Maximum Defense, attack in pairs, block the switch behind the wall,
# Mine Spreaders for the rest.
need: Force Field
need: Mine Spreader | Chain Gun | Rocket Launcher
recommended: Maximum Defense Booster
recommended: 4-Tires

== Red Star Mission 16 ==
# The aliens. Not in the walkthrough for Red Star (it describes the Terraflux way with Nitrogrenades):
# a guess, please check. 5 robos have to hold the big switch, a 6th goes through the last door.
need: 2 damage weapons
recommended: Oiler | Magneton
recommended: Speed Booster | Maximum Defense Booster


# ======================================================================== TERRAFLUX

== Terraflux Mission 1 ==
# Kill every enemy robo. Guide: 1x Laser lures the Homing Missiles out, 6x Eraser/Fire Frequency wait at the
# corridor.
# Hidden rule (game code): the mission is lost when 2 of your robos are destroyed. Tested 2026-10-20: Lasers,
# Whirlwinds, Nitrogrenades and Maximum Defense on Jet-Cars lost twice; the Erasers (shooting through the
# corridor's walls) are what makes it work.
# Won 2026-10-20 the guide's way (1 robo lost): 6x Eraser/Fire Frequency on the outside of the long vertical entrance
# corridor's right tube (they shoot a short way over it), the Laser walks down the corridor until the Homing
# Missiles come out, then back up past the Erasers. The Laser's Alien Defense Booster (pick it up on the way)
# wears off, so lure soon after picking it up.
need: Eraser
recommended: Laser

== Terraflux Mission 2 ==
# Chain Guns from the right, Homing Missiles from the center. Guide: 6+ Pulse Gun/Fire Frequency and a Laser.
need: Pulse Gun | Laser
recommended: Pulse Gun
recommended: Fire Frequency Booster | Shield

== Terraflux Mission 3 ==
# Hidden rule (game code): lost when a certain unit or building of yours is destroyed (not identified yet).
# The base is under fire at once. Guide: Pulse Gun/2-Legs/Fire Range ("must have"), faster and cheaper
# production.
need: Pulse Gun | Laser | Eraser
need: Fire Range Booster
recommended: Faster Production | Cheaper Production

== Terraflux Mission 4 ==
# The enemy base is behind a door opened by a switch; Kangaroos come over the pipes. Guide: 2x Buzzer/Spider
# and 2x Pulse Gun/Spider with Maximum Defense, 2x Pulse Gun/2-Legs, crush them in the door.
need: Pulse Gun
recommended: Buzzer
recommended: Spider Legs
recommended: Maximum Defense Booster
recommended: Cheaper Production

== Terraflux Mission 5 ==
# Two enemy bases. Guide: 3x Pulse Gun/Spider/Maximum Defense attack, 2x Buzzer/Spider/Range (or a
# Nitrogrenade) defend.
need: Pulse Gun
need: Buzzer | Nitrogrenade
recommended: Spider Legs
recommended: Maximum Defense Booster

== Terraflux Mission 6 ==
# $550 and three Rocket Launchers already destroying your base: one robo. Guide: Pulse Gun/Jet-Car/Maximum
# Defense, collect the three alien boosters.
need: Pulse Gun | Laser
recommended: Jet-Car
recommended: Maximum Defense Booster

== Terraflux Mission 7 ==
# Guide: 4x Pulse Gun/Jet-Car/Maximum Defense collect the 4 alien weapon boosters and shoot the spawning
# enemies; base energy +10% and regeneration.
need: Pulse Gun
recommended: Jet-Car
recommended: Maximum Defense Booster
recommended: Base Energy +10% | Base Regeneration

== Terraflux Mission 8 ==
# Two Homing Missiles attack. Guide: 1x Disrupter/Jet-Car/Speed catches them, 3x Buzzer/Spider/Maximum
# Defense attack through the tunnels.
need: Buzzer | Pulse Gun
recommended: Disrupter
recommended: Spider Legs

== Terraflux Mission 9 ==
# Guide: waves of 2x Buzzer/Teleporter/Maximum Defense jump right into the base; take the switch before the
# train hits your base again.
need: Buzzer | Pulse Gun
need: Teleporter | Jet-Pack
recommended: Maximum Defense Booster
recommended: Nitrogrenade | Disrupter

== Terraflux Mission 10 ==
# Guide: Pulse Gun/Teleporter/Maximum Defense jump into a 1-tile gap in the base's force field; keep
# Nitrogrenades home against Rocket Launchers.
need: Pulse Gun | Buzzer
need: Teleporter | Jet-Pack
recommended: Nitrogrenade
recommended: Maximum Defense Booster

== Terraflux Mission 11 ==
# No money: storm the castle with the robos you are given. Guide: buy the inventions before, Auto-Repair
# helps a lot here and in the next mission.
base: no
recommended: Robo Auto-Repair

== Terraflux Mission 12 ==
# No money, one robo: lure the Chain Guns into the acid, then shoot the base.
base: no
recommended: Robo Auto-Repair

== Terraflux Mission 13 ==
# Stop the convoy before it reaches the exit. Guide: 3-4x Disrupter/Hovercraft/Speed, Nitrogrenades, one weapon.
need: Disrupter | Nitrogrenade
need: damage weapon
recommended: Hovercraft | Jet-Car
recommended: Speed Booster

== Terraflux Mission 14 ==
# Your army is locked in. Guide: build 1 Jet-Pack/Speed robo and jump into the area with the switch.
need: Jet-Pack | Teleporter

== Terraflux Mission 15 ==
# Guide: Infector/Jet-Car/Camouflage (or Speed) straight to the base, kill teams of Disrupter/Nitrogrenade/Laser.
need: Infector
need: Disrupter | Nitrogrenade
recommended: Laser | Pulse Gun
recommended: Jet-Car
recommended: Camouflage | Speed Booster

== Terraflux Mission 16 ==
# The aliens. Guide: 6x Nitrogrenade/Jet-Car/Maximum Defense (or Speed); freeze the leading alien on the big
# switch, 5 robos hold the switch in the room, the 6th goes through the last door.
need: Nitrogrenade
recommended: Jet-Car
recommended: Maximum Defense Booster | Speed Booster
